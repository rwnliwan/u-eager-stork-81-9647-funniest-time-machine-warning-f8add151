A single warning label for a time machine, at most 120 characters, aiming for the biggest laugh, for the [make.fun competition](https://make.fun/c/u-eager-stork-81-9647-funniest-time-machine-warning-f8add151).

## Entry

> WARNING: Do not feed the dinosaurs. They remember you, and they hold grudges for 65 million years.

Run with Node 22: `node main.ts` writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$ENTRY_OUTPUT`).

## Craft

Straight-faced safety notice; a real time-travel hazard (dinosaurs) turned into the joke, surprise saved for the last words. Saturated angles avoided: meet/don't meet yourself, grandfather paradox, warranty, lend money to past self, "returns you to the exact moment you left", "do not change the past".
